RFM Customer Segmentation

Who are the best customers, and who is about to leave? Each customer is scored on Recency (days since last order), Frequency (number of orders) and Monetary value (total spend), then grouped into four segments.

Checking the segments with K-Means

K-Means clustering (k = 4) on the standardised R, F and M values was used as an independent check. Champions and Lost line up with a cluster; Loyal and At Risk are mixed, so the score-based segments are the clearer ones for business use.

How the scoring works

  • Each of R, F and M is scored 1–4 by quartile of the 5,881 customers. The cut-offs are .
  • The three scores are added (3–12): Champions 10–12, Loyal 7–9, At Risk 4–6, Lost 3.
  • Scores here are identical to the notebook's for every one of the customers (checked by an automated test), so the calculator and the analysis always agree.
