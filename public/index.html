Customer segmentation ·

Who are the best customers, and who is slipping away?

I scored each customer of a UK online retailer on how recently they ordered, how often they order and how much they spend, then grouped them into four segments.

K-Means (k = 4) on standardised R, F and M matches Champions and Lost but mixes Loyal and At Risk, so I kept the score-based segments.

06

Method

  1. Cleaning: removed rows without a customer ID, returns and cancellations, zero-price rows and duplicates.
  2. Recency, frequency and spend are each scored 1–4 by quartile. Cut-offs: .
  3. The three scores are added (3–12): Champions 10–12, Loyal 7–9, At Risk 4–6, Lost 3.
  4. The calculator matches the notebook for every customer (checked by npm test).
